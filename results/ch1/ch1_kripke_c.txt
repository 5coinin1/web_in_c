 WebSocket Chat Connection State Verification

Shared runtime state machine:

 STATE_IDLE
          | ACCEPT
          v
 STATE_HTTP_HANDSHAKE
          | HTTP_UPGRADE_OK
          v
 STATE_WS_CONNECTED
          | LOGIN_OK
          v
 STATE_AUTHENTICATED
          | JOIN_OK
          v
 STATE_IN_ROOM
          | LEAVE_OK / ROOM_CLOSED
          v
 STATE_AUTHENTICATED

 (switch rooms: LEAVE then JOIN - JOIN is only valid from
  AUTHENTICATED, i.e. a client must leave before joining another)
 (single session: a 2nd LOGIN for the same account fails -> LOGIN_FAIL,
  the connection stays in WS_CONNECTED)

 (any live state) --DISCONNECT--> STATE_CLOSED  (terminal)

Kripke model:

 K = (S, S0, R, L)

 S  = 6 connection states (IDLE..CLOSED)
 S0 = STATE_IDLE
 R  = client_state_next()
 L  = {WS_OPEN, AUTHENTICATED, IN_ROOM, CLOSED}

Safety properties:

 P1: AG(IN_ROOM -> AUTHENTICATED)
 P2: AG(AUTHENTICATED -> WS_OPEN)
 P3: AG(CLOSED -> !(WS_OPEN | AUTHENTICATED | IN_ROOM))

Liveness (EF reachability):

 L1: from every connected state, IN_ROOM or CLOSED is reachable
 L2: from IN_ROOM, AUTHENTICATED is reachable (leave possible)
